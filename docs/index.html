When can your own sunshine charge your EV?

你家屋頂的陽光，什麼時候能幫電動車充電？

SolarCast forecasts next-hour solar power for every corner of New Zealand and turns it into one decision for a small business: charge the EV now, or wait for more sun.

SolarCast 預測紐西蘭每個角落下一小時的太陽能發電量，並把它變成小店家每小時的一個決定：現在幫電動車充電，還是等更多陽光？

How it works運作方式

Our own machine-learning model makes every forecast. It only uses what is known at the end of the current hour, and it was tested once, on 16 months it had never seen.

每一個預測都來自我們自己的機器學習模型。它只用「這一小時結束時」已知的資訊，並且只在它從沒看過的 16 個月資料上測試一次。

1

Weather in

輸入天氣

Hourly sunlight, cloud, temperature and wind from Open-Meteo, plus its day-ahead forecast for the next hour (issued 24 hours earlier, so no peeking at the future).

Open-Meteo 的逐時日照、雲量、氣溫和風，加上它對下一小時的前一天預報（24 小時前就發布，所以沒有偷看未來）。

2

Forecast

預測

Two gradient-boosted tree models, one for sunlight and one for cloudiness (the clear-sky index), averaged. Trained 2022-2024, tuned on early 2025.

兩個梯度提升樹模型，一個預測日照、一個預測雲量（晴空指數），再取平均。用 2022–2024 年訓練，2025 年上半年調參。

3

Decision

決策

Predicted power of a 50 kW rooftop system becomes advice: charge the EV when solar covers the workshop and the car, and how much grid power, money and CO2 that saves.

把 50 kW 屋頂系統的預測發電量變成建議：太陽能夠供應店裡和車子時就充電，並算出省下多少電網用電、錢和 CO2。

v4 beats both baselines in every region. Weather is modelled (reanalysis) and PV output is derived with a stated formula, because New Zealand has no public hourly data for single solar sites; real plants would be harder to forecast.

v4 在每一個地區都贏過兩個基準方法。天氣是模擬（再分析）資料，發電量用明確公式換算，因為紐西蘭沒有公開的單一電廠逐時資料；真實電廠會更難預測。

AI you can trust with the numbers不亂編數字的 AI

On top of the forecast, an open language model (Qwen3) runs on the notebook's own machine. It writes a national briefing every hour and answers questions in English or Chinese, but it never computes or invents numbers.

在預測之上，開源語言模型（Qwen3）直接在 notebook 那台機器上執行。它每小時寫一段全國摘要，也用中英文回答問題，但它從不自己算數字或編數字。

  • Grounded: the server looks up the facts with the same code as the map; the model only explains them.
  • 有根據：伺服器用和地圖相同的程式查出數據，模型只負責解釋。
  • Offline: no internet, no tools, no API keys.
  • 離線：不能上網、沒有工具、不需要 API 金鑰。
  • Guarded: keyword and injection filters, the Llama Guard 3 safety classifier, topic rules and rate limits.
  • 有防護：關鍵字與提示注入過濾、Llama Guard 3 安全分類器、主題限制和頻率限制。
  • Honest: in replay mode it says the hour is in the past, and the page always shows which answers came from the AI.
  • 誠實：回放模式下會說明那是過去的時段，網頁也會標示哪些回答來自 AI。

On your own computer

Python 3.10 or newer. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 when it is ready.
