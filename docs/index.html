How much power will your panels give this hour?

你的太陽能板，這一小時能發多少電？

SolarCast tells anyone in New Zealand with rooftop solar how much power their panels will make this hour and the next two, so they can run the EV charger, hot water or machines on their own sunshine instead of buying from the grid.

The problem問題

More and more New Zealand homes, schools and businesses have rooftop solar. To get the most from it, they should run big loads (an EV charger, a hot-water cylinder, a heat pump, a workshop machine) in the hours when the sun is actually there, and buy less power from the grid. But clouds come and go, so it is hard to know how much power the panels will make in the next few hours. Weather apps show cloud and temperature, not kilowatts for your own roof. SolarCast answers one question for any spot in the country: how many kW will my panels give this hour?

紐西蘭越來越多家庭、學校和公司裝了屋頂太陽能。要用得最划算，就該把電動車充電、熱水器、熱泵或工廠機器排在真的有太陽的時候，少向電網買電。但雲飄來飄去，很難知道接下來幾小時太陽能板能發多少電。天氣 App 只告訴你雲量和氣溫，不會告訴你自己屋頂有幾 kW。SolarCast 回答一個問題：全國任何地點，我的太陽能板這一小時能發幾 kW？

What we built我們做了什麼

A dashboard that opens from a notebook (Colab or Kaggle) and gives a public link. Pick your panel size (3 to 100 kW) and click anywhere in New Zealand.

一個從 notebook（Colab 或 Kaggle）打開、並給你公開連結的儀表板。選好太陽能板大小（3 到 100 kW），再點紐西蘭任何地方就行。

  • Three modes: Replay the past 24 hours (forecast next to what really happened), Live now (the hour going on now) and Predict next (the next two hours, with how accurate each is).
  • 三種模式：回放過去 24 小時（預測和實際發電並排）、Live now（正在進行的這一小時）、Predict next（接下來兩小時，並標明各自準不準）。
  • Nationwide map: the same model for 137 land cells (about 50 km each), recomputed every hour.
  • 全國地圖：同一個模型涵蓋 137 個陸地格子（每格約 50 公里），每小時重算。
  • Charge your EV from your panels: pick one of about 40 EVs and plug-in hybrids sold in New Zealand, the battery level and the charger; it shows when the panels finish, the range added and the money saved. A home battery option (for example a Tesla Powerwall 3) shows how much left-over sun it could store for the evening.
  • 用太陽能板幫電動車充電：從紐西蘭有賣的約 40 款電動車和插電式油電混合車選你的車，設定電量和充電器；它會算出太陽能板什麼時候能充完、增加多少續航、省多少錢。加上家用電池（例如特斯拉 Powerwall 3），還能看到多出來的陽光能存多少給晚上用。
  • SolarCast AI: a chat that explains the numbers. It runs on the notebook's own GPU, with no internet or tools; the server looks up the facts with the same code as the map, and the model only explains them.
  • SolarCast AI：解釋數字的聊天助理。它跑在 notebook 自己的 GPU 上，不能上網也沒有任何工具；事實由伺服器用和地圖同一套程式查好，模型只負責解釋。

What we added. The suggested project forecasts one solar site one day ahead. We changed three things: the time (people decide what to run in the next few hours, so we forecast this hour and the next two, and tested how far ahead the forecast stays useful), the place and the user (any spot and any panel size in New Zealand), and the question behind the forecast (we measured whether it changes a real decision, not only whether it is accurate).

我們加了什麼。題目建議的做法是預測單一太陽能場站的隔天發電。我們改了三件事：時間（大家決定要開什麼是看接下來幾小時，所以我們預測這一小時和接下來兩小時，並測試預測能準到幾小時）、地點和使用者（紐西蘭任何地點、任何大小的系統），以及預測背後的問題（我們量測它會不會改變真實決策，而不只是準不準）。

How it works運作方式

Our own machine-learning model makes every forecast. It only uses what is known at the end of the current hour, and it was tested once, on 16 months it had never seen.

每一個預測都來自我們自己的機器學習模型。它只用「這一小時結束時」已知的資訊，並且只在它從沒看過的 16 個月資料上測試一次。

1

Weather in

輸入天氣

Hourly sunlight, cloud, temperature and wind from Open-Meteo, plus its day-ahead forecast for the next hour (issued 24 hours earlier, so no peeking at the future).

Open-Meteo 的逐時日照、雲量、氣溫和風，加上它對下一小時的前一天預報（24 小時前就發布，所以沒有偷看未來）。

2

Forecast

預測

Two gradient-boosted tree models, one for sunlight and one for cloudiness (the clear-sky index), averaged. Trained 2022-2024, tuned on early 2025.

兩個梯度提升樹模型，一個預測日照、一個預測雲量（晴空指數），再取平均。用 2022–2024 年訓練，2025 年上半年調參。

3

Your panels

你的太陽能板

Sunlight becomes power for the panel size you choose (3 to 100 kW): next hour in kW, the day in kWh and what it is worth. Worked example: a workshop charges its EV when solar covers the load and the car.

把日照換算成你選的系統大小（3 到 100 kW）的發電量：下一小時幾 kW、整天幾度電、值多少錢。應用範例：太陽能夠供應店裡和車子時，加工店就幫電動車充電。

See where the power goes看見電往哪裡流

When you pick a spot on the dashboard, an animated picture shows the next hour: sunlight hits your panels and the power flows into your home. The more of their rated power the panels give, the brighter the sun and the faster the power moves. When the worked example says "charge now", the power flows on to the EV. At night the home runs on the grid.

在儀表板上選一個地點，就會看到下一小時的動畫：陽光照到你的太陽能板，電流進家裡。發電量佔額定功率越高，太陽越亮、電流跑得越快。應用範例建議「現在充電」時，電會繼續流到電動車；晚上則改由電網供電。

Charge your EV from your panels. Pick your car from about 40 EVs and plug-in hybrids sold in New Zealand (type a brand or model to find it: Tesla Model 3 and Y in every version, the older Model S and X, BYD including the Sealion 5, Sealion 6 and Shark 6 plug-in hybrids, MG, Kia, Hyundai, Polestar, Volvo, Toyota, Nissan Leaf), the battery level now and the target. From the hourly forecast the dashboard works out when your panels would finish, or how far they get today and what is still needed, the range added and the money saved, next to how long the same charge takes from the grid. An animated battery fills up and bars show where each hour's solar goes. Add a home battery such as a Tesla Powerwall 3 to see how much left-over sun it stores for the evening, for the car and the home.

Replay past 24 h | Live now | Predict next. Replay the last 24 hours to compare our forecast with what really happened, see the hour going on now, or predict the next hour and the hour after it. We stop there: counted from the latest weather data, on unseen test months SolarCast beats the weather service's own forecast by 40% at 1 hour, 16% at 2 hours and 6% at 3 hours, and not at all from 6 hours.

Replay past 24 h｜Live now｜Predict next。回放過去 24 小時，對照我們的預測和實際發電；即時看現在正在進行的這一小時；或預測下一小時和再下一小時。只做到這裡：從最新天氣資料往後算，在模型沒看過的測試期，SolarCast 比天氣服務自己的預報好 40%（1 小時）、16%（2 小時）、6%（3 小時），6 小時以後就沒有比較好。

The dashboard opens on the sunniest region right now, so the animation is there without a click. Example below: a 5 kW home system.

用你的太陽能板幫電動車充電。從紐西蘭有賣的約 40 款電動車和插電式油電混合車選你的車（打品牌或車名就能找到：特斯拉 Model 3、Y 各版本，較舊的 Model S、X，比亞迪（包括 Sealion 5、Sealion 6、Shark 6 插電式油電混合車）、MG、Kia、Hyundai、Polestar、Volvo、Toyota、Nissan Leaf），再設定現在的電量和要充到多少。儀表板依每小時預測算出太陽能板什麼時候能充完，或今天能充到哪裡、還差多少，增加多少續航、省多少錢，並和改用電網要充多久比較。電池會有充電動畫，長條圖顯示每小時的太陽能用在哪裡。加上家用電池（例如特斯拉 Powerwall 3），就能看到白天多的太陽能能存多少，晚上給車子和家裡用。

儀表板一打開就會選好目前最晴朗的地區，不用點也看得到動畫。下方範例：家用 5 kW 系統。

Results成果

Test period July 2025 to October 2026 (16 months the model never saw), daytime hours, all 14 regions. Average error of the next-hour forecast, lower is better. Output scales with panel size, so the error as a share of capacity is the same for a 5 kW home and a 50 kW business.

測試期 2025 年 7 月到 2026 年 10 月（模型沒看過的 16 個月），白天時段，全部 14 個地區。下一小時預測的平均誤差，越小越好。發電量和板子大小成正比，所以「誤差佔容量的比例」對家用 5 kW 和商用 50 kW 都一樣。

v4 is 32.6% better than clear-sky persistence, 40.3% better than Open-Meteo's own day-ahead forecast, and beats both baselines in every region (14/14). The same error is 7.9% of the average daytime output. Weather is modelled (reanalysis) and PV output is derived with a stated formula, because New Zealand has no public hourly data for single solar sites; real plants would be harder to forecast.

v4 比晴空 Persistence 好 32.6%、比 Open-Meteo 自己的前一天預報好 40.3%，並在每一個地區都贏過兩個基準方法（14/14）。同樣的誤差是白天平均發電量的 7.9%。天氣是模擬（再分析）資料，發電量用明確公式換算，因為紐西蘭沒有公開的單一電廠逐時資料；真實電廠會更難預測。

Can it get better? We tried (v5, judged on the validation period only, with a bar set in advance: at least 1 W/m² better than v4): inputs for how wrong the day-ahead forecast is right now, the forecast's trend, and five tuning variants. The best gained only 0.26 W/m² (28.98 → 28.71), so v4 stays and the test period was not looked at again. Then v6 used the GPU: XGBoost trained on a Colab T4, 48 settings plus a blend with v4. Best 28.56 W/m² (gain 0.42), still below the bar. The error left seems to come from the weather data itself, not the model.

還能更準嗎？我們試過（v5，只看驗證期，門檻事先訂好：要比 v4 好至少 1 W/m²）：加入「前一天預報現在錯了多少」、預報的變化趨勢，以及五組參數調整。最好的只進步 0.26 W/m²（28.98 → 28.71），所以保留 v4，也沒有再去看測試期。接著 v6 改用 GPU：在 Colab T4 上訓練 XGBoost，試了 48 組設定，再和 v4 混合。最好是 28.56 W/m²（進步 0.42），還是沒過門檻。剩下的誤差看起來主要來自天氣資料本身，而不是模型。

AI you can trust with the numbers不亂編數字的 AI

On top of the forecast, an open language model (Qwen3) runs on the notebook's own machine. It writes a national briefing every hour and answers questions in English or Chinese, but it never computes or invents numbers.

在預測之上，開源語言模型（Qwen3）直接在 notebook 那台機器上執行。它每小時寫一段全國摘要，也用中英文回答問題，但它從不自己算數字或編數字。

  • Grounded: the server looks up the facts with the same code as the map; the model only explains them.
  • 有根據：伺服器用和地圖相同的程式查出數據，模型只負責解釋。
  • Offline: no internet, no tools, no API keys.
  • 離線：不能上網、沒有工具、不需要 API 金鑰。
  • Guarded: keyword and injection filters, the Llama Guard 3 safety classifier, topic rules and rate limits.
  • 有防護：關鍵字與提示注入過濾、Llama Guard 3 安全分類器、主題限制和頻率限制。
  • Size-aware: every number is already scaled to your panel size before the model sees it. Tested on a T4: a 5 kW home in Christchurch gets 3.36 kW next hour, the 50 kW workshop example 33.6 kW.
  • 依系統大小：交給模型的每個數字都已換算成你的系統大小。在 T4 上實測：基督城家用 5 kW 下一小時 3.36 kW，50 kW 加工店範例 33.6 kW。
  • Honest: in replay mode it says the hour is in the past, and the page always shows which answers came from the AI.
  • 誠實：回放模式下會說明那是過去的時段，網頁也會標示哪些回答來自 AI。

Where it fails哪裡會出錯

  • Cloudy hours are much harder. The error is 15.8 W/m² when cloud cover is at most 60%, and 41.1 W/m² above that.
  • 多雲的小時難很多。雲量不超過 60% 時誤差是 15.8 W/m²，超過就變成 41.1 W/m²。
  • Modelled weather, not real panels. We had no measured output from real New Zealand solar sites. Modelled weather is smoother than reality, so a real rooftop would be harder to forecast than our numbers suggest.
  • 用的是模擬天氣，不是真的太陽能板。我們沒有紐西蘭真實場站的實測發電量。模擬的天氣比現實平滑，所以真實屋頂會比我們的數字更難預測。
  • Our validation rule was too loose for longer horizons. On the validation months every horizon up to 24 hours passed our rule (at least 10% better than the weather service), but on the test months only 1-2 hours were clearly better. We report the test result and only offer this hour and the next two.
  • 我們的驗證規則對較長的預測時距太寬鬆。在驗證月份，直到 24 小時的每個時距都過了我們的門檻（至少比天氣服務好 10%），但在測試月份只有 1–2 小時明顯更好。我們報告的是測試結果，所以只提供這一小時和接下來兩小時。
  • Two later model versions did not help. New inputs and a GPU-trained XGBoost model gained less than our bar on validation, so we kept the simpler model. The error left seems to be mostly in the weather data itself.
  • 後來的兩個模型版本沒有更好。新增輸入和用 GPU 訓練的 XGBoost 在驗證集上的進步都沒達到門檻，所以我們保留較簡單的模型。剩下的誤差似乎大多來自天氣資料本身。
  • The AI chat can still be wrong. A small language model can misread the facts, and no safety filter is perfect. This is a prototype: the numbers are estimates, not advice to buy equipment.
  • AI 聊天仍然可能出錯。小型語言模型可能會誤讀事實，也沒有任何安全過濾器是完美的。這是原型：數字只是估計，不是買設備的建議。

What we'd do next下一步

  • Test against real output from New Zealand homes and schools with solar, with their permission.
  • 在對方同意下，用紐西蘭有太陽能的家庭和學校的真實發電量來測試。
  • Add real household loads and power prices that change by the hour, so the advice says when to run each appliance.
  • 加入真實的家庭用電和隨小時變動的電價，讓建議能說出每個電器該什麼時候開。
  • Try SolarCast with a few families and a school, and find out whether it changes when they charge and heat.
  • 讓幾個家庭和一所學校試用 SolarCast，看看它是否改變他們充電和燒熱水的時間。
